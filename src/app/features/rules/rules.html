Scramble Roulette is a golf bachelor party scramble game that adds a deck of cards to standard scramble format golf and pits two teams against each other. Each team plays its own round and draws its own cards. These rules govern every hole — when to draw, how team cards work, and what happens when the beer cart rolls up mid-challenge. Lowest team score wins.
